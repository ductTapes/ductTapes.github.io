Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my recordings saved?

All videos are saved directly to your device’s Photos / Camera Roll. TwoFrame does not store anything on external servers.

Why does TwoFrame need camera and microphone access?

Camera access is required to record video. Microphone access is required to capture audio in your recordings. TwoFrame uses these only when you are actively recording — nothing is captured in the background.

Does TwoFrame collect any personal data?

No. TwoFrame does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

The app crashes on launch — what do I do?

Try the following: (1) Restart your iPhone. (2) Delete and reinstall TwoFrame. (3) Ensure your iOS version is up to date. If the issue persists, contact us.

Can I record in 4K?

Recording quality depends on your device’s hardware capabilities and iOS settings. TwoFrame uses the native camera API and supports the resolutions available on your device.
