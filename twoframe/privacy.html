Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 28, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TwoFrame (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our iOS application. TwoFrame is developed and operated by Yury Hryshchanka, an individual developer.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

TwoFrame does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not store any videos or photos on our servers — all media remains exclusively on your device.
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles.

3. Camera and Microphone Access

TwoFrame requires access to your device’s camera and microphone solely to provide its core functionality: recording simultaneous portrait and landscape video. This data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted externally.

4. Data Storage

All video content recorded through TwoFrame is saved directly to your device’s Camera Roll / Photos library. We have no access to this content whatsoever.

5. Third-Party Services

TwoFrame does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services.

6. Children’s Privacy

TwoFrame does not knowingly collect any information from users of any age. Since no data is collected at all, the App is safe for use by all age groups.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date above. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
